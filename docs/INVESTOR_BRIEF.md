# Investor brief & 15-minute demo script

**Emirattest** — *Documents cleared. Life moves.* A single, accountable platform for attestation, PRO, relocation and resident setup in the UAE, serving individuals, families, employers, typing centres/agencies, providers and field agents across web, Android and WhatsApp.

## The pitch in five lines
1. **Problem** — opaque routes and prices, scattered family/employer records, fulfilment run on phone calls with no custody evidence.
2. **Product** — purpose-based intake → versioned route registry → itemised approved quotation → provider choice with visible track record → tracked custody → completion pack; reusable family and employer records with consent.
3. **Trust by design** — estimate ≠ quote ≠ variation, official charges ≠ retained revenue, no guaranteed outcomes, no authenticity claims from a stamp or QR, dual-approval refunds, hash-chained audit.
4. **Economics (plan v2.0, illustrative)** — AED 81,480 net retained revenue/month at the reference mix, AED 36,130 contribution, AED 6,130 operating surplus after AED 30,000 fixed overhead; breakeven ≈ AED 67,656 revenue (≈ 83% of reference contribution). Funding envelope AED 330,000 (setup 80k, six-month liquidity 180k, supplier timing 40k, contingency 30k).
5. **Ask** — fund the production IT structure (security, infrastructure, live payments/WhatsApp, partner integrations, native release, operations team) summarised on `/investors`.

## Likely investor questions
* **Is this live?** No — see `REAL_VS_SIMULATED.md`. Nothing is claimed that is not built.
* **What stops a rival copying features?** The plan itself says exclusivity is not claimed; the defensible assets are the maintained registry, accountable fulfilment data, and permissioned family/employer records.
* **Regulatory exposure?** Every regulated category is delivered through authorised partners with disclosures; the platform neither holds escrow-like funds nor offers credit as a financial product.
* **Unit economics?** Mix-based and shown transparently; the funding model includes supplier timing and refund exposure.
